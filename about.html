Jana Jagriti Secondary School

A centre of learning, knowledge and practical education.

Jana Jagriti Secondary School is located at Lamkichuha Municipality-5, Chauri, Kailali, Sudurpashchim Province, Nepal.

The school provides quality education from Playgroup to Grade 12 with general and technical education programs.

The school offers Science, Management, Education, Computer Science and Civil Engineering streams.
