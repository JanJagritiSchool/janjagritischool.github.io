About Jana Jagriti Secondary School

Jana Jagriti Secondary School is a reputed educational institution located at Lamkichuha-5, Chauri, Kailali, Sudurpashchim Province, Nepal.

Established in 2034 B.S., the school has been continuously working to provide quality education with modern teaching methods, discipline and practical learning opportunities.

The school provides education from Play Group to Grade 12 and offers academic as well as technical programs to prepare students for higher education and professional careers.

Principal

Mr. Puskar Singh Bohara

The school is guided by dedicated leadership and committed teachers who focus on academic excellence, innovation and the overall development of students.

We believe that every student has unique potential. Our goal is to create a supportive learning environment where students can grow with confidence and responsibility.
