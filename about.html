About Our School

Jana Jagriti Secondary School - Quality Education Is Our Commitment

Jana Jagriti Secondary School

Jana Jagriti Secondary School is a leading educational institution located at Lamkichuha-5, Chauri, Kailali, Sudurpashchim Province, Nepal.

Established with a vision to provide quality education, the school has been continuously working to develop knowledgeable, skilled and responsible citizens through modern and practical teaching methods.

The school provides education from Playgroup (PG) to Grade 12 with academic and technical programs including Civil Engineering, Science, Management, Education, Arts and Computer Science streams.
