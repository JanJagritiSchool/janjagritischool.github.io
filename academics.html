Our Academic Programs

Jana Jagriti Secondary School provides quality education with academic and technical programs designed for students' overall development.

Playgroup - Grade 5

Foundation education with child-friendly learning activities and strong basic skills development.

Grade 6 - 8

Basic level education focusing on knowledge, creativity and practical learning.

Grade 9 - 10

Secondary level education with academic excellence and career preparation.

Grade 11 - 12

Higher secondary programs with different streams according to students' interests.

Grade 11 & 12 Programs

Science Stream

Quality science education with theoretical knowledge and practical laboratory experience.

Management Stream

Business, accounting and management education for future career opportunities.

Education Stream

Developing skilled learners with knowledge of teaching and educational practices.

Arts Stream

Humanities education focusing on society, culture and creative thinking.

Computer Science

Modern computer education with programming and technology-based learning.

Civil Engineering

Technical education with practical skills, surveying and civil engineering laboratory experience.
