Welcome to Jana Jagriti Secondary School

Jana Jagriti Secondary School is a leading educational institution committed to providing quality education from Play Group to Grade 12. The school focuses on academic excellence, practical learning, discipline, creativity and character development.

Our Technical Stream in Civil Engineering and +2 programs in Science, Management, Education, Arts and Computer Science provide students with opportunities to build a successful future.
