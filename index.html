Welcome to Jana Jagriti Secondary School

A centre of learning dedicated to academic excellence, character building and practical education.

Jana Jagriti Secondary School, located at Lamkichuha Municipality-5, Chauri, Kailali, is committed to providing quality education from Playgroup to Grade 12 with modern teaching approaches and a student-friendly learning environment.

The school offers general and technical education including Civil Engineering, Science, Management, Education and Computer Science streams.
