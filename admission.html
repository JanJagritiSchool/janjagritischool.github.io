Begin your learning journey at Jana Jagriti Secondary School

Admission Open

Admissions are open for the new academic session. We welcome students from Playgroup (PG) to Grade 12 in both general and technical streams.

Eligibility

Admissions are available from Playgroup (PG) to Grade 12 according to the admission policy and previous academic qualifications.

Required Documents

• Birth Certificate
• Previous Marksheet
• Character Certificate (if applicable)
• Passport Size Photos

Admission Process

Fill out the admission form, submit the required documents, complete verification, and receive admission confirmation.

Important Notice

Seats are limited. Admissions will be provided on a first-come, first-served basis after verification.
